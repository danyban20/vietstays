Vietstays · Host Dashboard

Progress Report

A plain-language overview of what has been built so far, aligned with the Host Dashboard design and product requirements.

Date: 7 September 2026 Status: Published to production

What this project is

We are rebuilding the Vietstays host portal — the tool property managers use to run apartments, bookings, guests, team members, and settings. The interactive design prototype shows how each screen should look and behave; this report describes what is already working in the live product.

Completed & live

Completed features

Getting in and navigation

  • Login for hosts and administrators
  • Main menu (sidebar) with dashboard layout
  • Dashboard home with a business summary

Apartments & bookings

  • All bookings — searchable list with filters (dates, area, apartment, status, and more)
  • Booking calendar — visual timeline by apartment; bookings can be moved by drag-and-drop
  • Booking details — view and edit guest and payment information
  • Add booking — manual booking, block dates, and external booking (Airbnb, Booking.com, etc.)
  • All apartments — list of properties
  • Apartment details — availability, pricing, and presentation tabs
  • Add new apartment — step-by-step setup flow

Customers

  • Customer list built from real booking data
  • Tabs: all, needs action, staying now, upcoming, returning
  • Search, filter, sort, and summary statistics
  • Customer profile with stay history and segment (VIP, returning, new)
  • Add new customer — only name required; email and phone optional
  • Temporary customers for guests without email (can book now, link to real account later)
  • Go straight to reservation after creating a customer
  • Customer count shown in the sidebar

My team — Sales team

  • List of hosts, co-hosts, and agents with tabs and performance overview
  • Invite to sales team — email, role, area, commission setup, and access rights
  • Invitations tab — pending invites; withdraw an invitation

My team — Operations team

  • List of helpers (cleaning, keys, courier, cash collection)
  • Privacy notice — helpers see tasks, not guest dialogue (as in the design)
  • Guest info toggle per person — choose whether they see guest name and phone
  • Add helper — email or phone, role, area, and access rights
  • Invitations tab for pending helpers

Management company

  • Empty-state explanation of what a management company is
  • 4-step setup wizard — name & legal form, apartments, invite hosts, revenue split
  • Active company view — members, apartments, and revenue model (works in the browser as a demo; not yet saved permanently)

Settings & administration

  • Email settings and email templates
  • Language settings
  • Administrator user management
  • Host application review (people applying to become hosts)

Public website (guest-facing)

  • Home and apartment search
  • Apartment detail pages
  • Online booking and checkout
  • Host application form for new property owners

Summary

Strong progress on the heart of the product: bookings, apartments, customers, and both team types, closely following the design. The add-customer, invite-to-sales-team, and add-helper flows from the design are implemented and published. Still ahead: several menu areas from the full design, deeper customer and team workflows, and permanent storage for management companies.
